# Coaching rules

Standing rules for analysing workouts and writing/adjusting the plan and
Garmin workouts. Agreed with Dima over past sessions; they override
generic training advice. Goal, HR zones, checkpoints and the progress log
live in [Half_Marathon_Training_Plan_May2027.md](Half_Marathon_Training_Plan_May2027.md).

  doubtful data. For HR-based comparisons rely on data from 29.09 onward.
- Session without the strap (wrist HR): don't use its HR or Garmin TE for
  comparisons — wrist HR lags on intervals and can lock onto cadence
  (07.11.2026: "peaks" 172-178 ≈ cadence 175-181). Judge by reps/speed + RPE.
- Keep HRmax at 188 in the plan unless Dima asks (old strap showed 190).
- Don't use Garmin's race predictor (10k 31:40 on 28.09.26 is unrealistic).
- Races: Riga 5k 19.09.2026 — use the **chip time 18:02** (3:36/km), not
  the watch GPS (18:48 / 5.03 km).
- At a checkpoint, if Z3 isn't on target, shift later-phase paces by

## Treadmill

### No pace comparison

Never compare treadmill (`treadmill_running`) paces with outdoor runs, the
baseline, or the progress log — unless Dima explicitly says so for that
run. Indoors (21-22°C, no airflow) HR is higher at the same pace and
treadmill/watch pace is inaccurate (e.g. 23.09.2026 treadmill 5:31/km @
HR 138 vs 25.09 outdoor 5:11/km @ 136). Treadmill HR-based sessions are
fine to judge by HR; just leave pace out ("treadmill, not compared").

### Intervals (500 / 800 m)

- 1% incline, speeds in **km/h**, all steps **by time** — watch distance
  on the treadmill is unreliable and Dima thinks in treadmill km/h.
- Warm-up 20' and cool-down 10' @ 10.4 km/h; jog recovery 2:35 @ 11.6 km/h.
- Garmin workouts: time steps, km/h in the step description.
- Dima raises speed mid-session himself when it feels controlled.
- History:
  - 24.10.2026 8×500 @ 14.3 (HR peaks 157→171)
  - 01.11.2026 8×800 (3:08): rep 1 @ 15.2, 2-3 @ 15.3, 4-8 @ 15.4;
    peaks 166→173
  - 07.11.2026 10×800 (3:08): rep 1 @ 15.3, 2-10 @ 15.4; felt hard.
    Wrist HR only (no strap) — HR not usable
- Next 800s: stay at 15.4 until 10 reps feel controlled; progress reps
  or speed, not both.

### Hills (200 m reps)

- 5% incline, speed in **km/h**, rep by time (200 m @ 19 km/h ≈ 38 s).
- Warm-up / cool-down by time and HR (~20' @ 10-10.4 km/h), not distance.
- Dima picks the rep count by overall load — more than the plan's 8-10 is
  intentional. **Cap: 16 reps.**
- Progress by speed (19 → 19.5 km/h) or incline (5 → 6%), not reps.
- Jog recovery (7-7.9 km/h) noticeably raises overall load vs walking
  recovery (7 km/h long stride) — account for it when comparing sessions.
- History:
  - 24.09.2026 14 reps; 30.09 16 reps, 18.4 → 19 km/h
  - 08.10 16 @ 19, jog recovery, peaks 174-178 (last 180), hard
  - 15.10 14 @ 19.5, peaks 176-181
  - 22.10 10 @ 19.5, walk recovery, peaks 175-180 — 19.5 mastered
  - 05.11 12 @ 19.5: reps 1-8 @ 5%, 9-12 @ 6% (self-raised), peaks
    170-176; 6% didn't raise HR
- Next: 19.5 km/h @ 6%.

## Bike

On the bike Dima is limited by **leg strength, not cardio** — he's a
runner; e.g. 26.09.2026 8×3:00 @ 212-227 W felt hard at only HR 133-152.

- Don't extrapolate FTP from HR. Realistic FTP ~205-215 W (20' @ 200 W in
  May 2026); Garmin has 178 W (set manually, Dec 2025).
- Set watts by RPE and actual power; use HR only as an upper cap.
- To raise cardio load without overloading legs, raise cadence (95-105).
